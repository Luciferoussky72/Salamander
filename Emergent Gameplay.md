1. There should always be more than one way to solve a problem. They should not necessarily all be equal in pros and cons, either.

2. The player's strategy should not be fixed. A game like tic-tac-toe; where the strategy is fixed; is boring.

3. There should be no "useless" mechanics or items in general. Mostly-useless or joke items or mechanics are fine though, 
as long as they are kept to a minimum and can still get a chuckle out of players.

4. Keep RNG and other forms of artificial difficulty to a minimum. Long-story-short; artificial difficulty is not fun. 
The player should fail because the player failed, not because a random number generator failed to be favorable to them.
